5 Lola Blanket dupes worth a look.

A sculpted faux-fur throw can make a worn leather sofa feel like the best seat in the house. The right alternative depends on whether you want Lola’s pillowy texture, its generous size, or the stretch that makes it so easy to wrap around yourself.

This article contains paid links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

What makes Lola different?

The Original in Large measures 60 × 72 inches and uses a 95% polyester, 5% spandex faux fur. The stretch and plush feel on both sides are the features to compare. Plenty of bubble throws give you that sculpted look on a sofa. For the same wraparound feel, look for a similar size and a fabric that explicitly lists stretch.
