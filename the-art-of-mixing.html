AN INTERIOR PHILOSOPHY · VOL. 01

The Art
of Mixing.

A little Oakland Hills in 1974. A little Milan in 1982. Something handmade, something weathered, something wonderfully strange. The best rooms never look like they came from one store.

01 / THE BONES

Start with California.

Wood, daylight, plants, low furniture, warm earth tones. Think a redwood house tucked into the hills: architectural, relaxed, and open to the landscape.

02 / THE INTERRUPTION

Add something Italian.

A tubular chrome chair. A lacquer-red table. An oversized mushroom lamp. A glossy, slightly absurd object that interrupts all the natural texture.

03 / THE SOUL

Let things be imperfect.

Wabi-sabi brings in the patina: a chipped handmade bowl, uneven glaze, weathered timber, a vintage rug with a story. Don't polish away the character.

04 / THE RULE

Don't match. Converse.

Pair chrome with oak. Velvet with terracotta. A 1980s lamp with a 19th-century table. Repeat a color or material to make the contrast feel intentional.
